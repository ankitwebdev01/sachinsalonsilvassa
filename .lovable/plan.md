# Sachin Unisex Salon Website

## Goal
Build a five-page salon website closely matching Enrich Beauty’s clean, image-led structure, while using Sachin Unisex Salon’s identity, uploaded work, contact details, and Silvassa-focused content.

## Public website
- **Home:** Enrich-inspired header, offer-led opening area, service shortcuts, salon highlights, ratings, and WhatsApp booking actions.
- **Services:** Women’s and men’s service categories with editable names, descriptions, and prices.
- **Products:** Curated salon-care product showcase in the same browsing style; no checkout unless added later.
- **Salons:** Silvassa location, full contact details, opening information, map link, photo gallery, reviews, FAQ, and WhatsApp contact.
- **Membership:** Salon offers and membership benefits with a WhatsApp enquiry action.
- Shared navigation and footer, including the address, phone number, social links, and WhatsApp contact.
- Use the supplied logo as the brand mark and favicon, and the supplied salon photos in the gallery.
- Create additional original salon imagery where needed so the design feels complete without copying Enrich’s copyrighted photos.

## Content and search visibility
- Adapt the supplied About, Summary, FAQ, and review content into polished sections.
- Add natural, useful Silvassa-focused search copy covering at least 50 relevant salon phrases, without displaying a spam-like keyword dump.
- Give every public page distinct search and social metadata.
- Add salon structured data, review details supplied by the client, descriptive image text, and clear page headings.

## Contact behavior
- Every displayed phone number and primary contact action opens a personal WhatsApp chat with **+91 91734 14508**.
- Contact surfaces will show the business name, Silvassa address, phone number, and available links in an Enrich-inspired layout.

## Private admin area
- Enable Lovable Cloud for secure owner sign-in and persistent content.
- Use one secure admin account without a separate profile record, as requested.
- Add a protected dashboard to edit business name, address, contact details, services, prices, offers, products, gallery items, page copy, and sales entries.
- Public pages read the saved content, while sensible starter content is included immediately.

## Visual direction
- Closely follow Enrich Beauty’s restrained editorial layout: white surfaces, black typography, compact navigation, soft grey content tiles, rounded media, horizontally browsable collections, and sparse red accents.
- Keep Sachin’s logo and real salon photography prominent, with polished mobile and desktop layouts.
- Add subtle transitions only where they support navigation and browsing.

## Publishing details
- Disable the published “Edit with Lovable” badge through the project’s publishing setting rather than fragile page CSS.
- Keep the website unpublished until the owner chooses to publish.

## Verification
- Check all five public pages, private admin access, editing and persistence, WhatsApp links, mobile layout, metadata, images, and current build health.
